Following up on the successful deployment of the pilot plant in Lake Elementaita, Nakuru County, we proceeded with the design and development of the commercial plant.

The design focused on modularity allowing for the plant to be containerised and completely built in a factory versus being assembled in the field. This allows for fast and rapid deployments with very low capex.

Africa's first Direct Air Capture pilot plant. Achieved first CO₂ capture two months from project kickoff. Commercial-scale plant rated at 30,000 tonnes CO₂/year, taken from Design Verification Test (DVT) gate through Production Verification Test (PVT) to deployment-ready handover.
