Led the design and development of the Roam Move, an electric bus specifically engineered for urban mobility in Sub-Saharan Africa (the matatu sector). The project navigated unique design challenges including passenger capacity optimisation, robust construction for variable road conditions, and modular battery solutions suited to limited charging infrastructure.

15+ Units produced with over 200 pre-orders.

Sub-Saharan Africa's first locally designed and developed electric bus. Led product strategy for the follow-on Roam Rapid mass-transit bus.
