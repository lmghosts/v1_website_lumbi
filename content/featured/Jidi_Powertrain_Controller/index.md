Led the powertrain-controller programme for Kofa's Jidi e-motorcycle platform, owning field-oriented motor control (FOC) from tuning through phased fleet deployment. Work covered dq-frame current control, field-weakening behaviour, and the full torque envelope on a mid-drive PMSM powertrain.

Defined three use-case-tuned operating modes balancing range against performance, alongside a rider-facing feature set. The rollout shipped as a complete deployment package — rider documentation, installer SOPs, change logging, and partner coordination — taking a firmware upgrade cleanly from engineering to the field.

Phased rollout completed across approximately 300 commercial e-motorcycles in Ghana.

Delivered a 25% range increase and three use-case-tuned operating modes to a live commercial fleet.
