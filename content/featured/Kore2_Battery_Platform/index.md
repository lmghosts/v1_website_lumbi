Architected Kofa's next-generation swappable battery platform, engineered for the demands of high-cycle commercial fleet use across Kenya and Ghana. The programme spanned cell selection, pack-level electrical and thermal requirements, injection-moulded structural enclosures, and edge-hardware definition for fleet telemetry.

The design pursued parallel dual-sourcing across two qualified assemblers and both LFP and LMFP cell chemistries, de-risking supply while driving down cost. Enclosure design targeted an IP65 minimum ingress rating with an engineered elastomer sealing system, held accountable through a formal verification matrix.

Next-generation platform now in development across two vehicle and infrastructure programmes.

Targeting a 20% unit-cost reduction (~$150 per battery) and a 67% cycle-life increase, from 1,500 to 2,500 cycles.
