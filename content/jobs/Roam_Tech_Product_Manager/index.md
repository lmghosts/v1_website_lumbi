Led technical product management across four automotive product programmes: the Roam Air motorcycle, the Roam Move 32-seater matatu-class bus, the Roam Rapid low-floor mass-transit bus, and Griffin electric conversion kits. Directed cross-functional teams of more than 30 engineers and designers across battery development, electrical, mechanical, software, and reliability engineering.

- Owned full New Product Introduction cycles from Engineering Validation Test through Design Verification Test, Production Verification Test, and mass production for the Roam Air motorcycle, Roam Move bus, and Griffin conversion kits.
- Delivered Sub-Saharan Africa's first locally designed and developed electric motorcycle — the Roam Air — with 2,500 units in production. The Roam Air earned Roam Electric a 2022 Earthshot Prize Finalist designation (Prince William / The Royal Foundation — one of 15 global finalists). Roam was also named to the 'World's Most Innovative Companies' list during this period.
- Co-developed Kenya's national electric vehicle standard with the Kenya Bureau of Standards (KEBS) for nationwide implementation. Owned UN38.3 and UNECE R136 type-approval and certification.
- Delivered Sub-Saharan Africa's first locally designed and developed electric bus, the Roam Move, and led product strategy and development for the follow-on Roam Rapid low-floor mass-transit bus.
- Overhauled the product requirements specification process, reducing component manufacturing errors by 75% over two years (measured through aftersales failure rates and supplier-quality improvement).
